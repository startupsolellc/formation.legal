1. The $25,000 Paperwork Error

Most founders think penalties are for people who hide money. That is a mistake. The $25,000 penalty for Form 5472 is an "Information Reporting" penalty.

If you own a Single-Member LLC from outside the US, you are a "Foreign-Owned Disregarded Entity." If you fail to file Form 5472 with a pro-forma 1120: even if you have zero income: the IRS starts the clock at twenty-five thousand dollars.

We see this happen every year. Founders ignore the mail because they "did not make any money," only to find their US business dream ended by a single missing form.

2. FinCEN and the End of "Anonymous" LLCs

The Corporate Transparency Act is no longer a "future" problem. It is here. The BOI (Beneficial Ownership Information) report is a federal mandate.

If you are a global founder, you are likely a "Reporting Company." This is not a tax filing; it is a transparency requirement. The fines for non-compliance are not just financial; they can include criminal charges.

3. The "Zero-Tax" Illusion

You have probably heard that "US LLCs are tax-free for non-residents." This is a dangerous half-truth.

While you might not owe US income tax on foreign-source income, the IRS still monitors your "Transfer Pricing." Under Section 482, the IRS has the power to reallocate your income if they believe you are moving profits out of the US unfairly.

Without a clear compliance framework, your "Zero-Tax" structure is a target, not a shield.
