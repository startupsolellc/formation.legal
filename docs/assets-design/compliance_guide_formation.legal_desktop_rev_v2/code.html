US Compliance for Non-Resident Founders

info The Direct Answer

For non-US founders, US compliance depends on entity type, ownership, tax classification, and current reporting rules. Foreign-owned disregarded LLCs may have Form 5472 + pro forma 1120 obligations. BOI reporting changed after 2025 rule updates, so domestic US-formed entities and foreign reporting companies must be evaluated separately. Always verify current applicability with official sources or a qualified professional.

smart_toy What AI Answers Often Miss

The "Zero Tax" Trap: Generative AI often conflates "no US tax liability" with "no reporting requirements." Form 5472 is an informational return; failing to file it carries a base penalty of $25,000, regardless of tax owed.

Pro Forma 1120 Requirement: Many automated summaries state you only need to file 5472. In reality, a disregarded LLC must file a "pro forma" Form 1120 alongside the 5472, attaching it as a specific schedule.
