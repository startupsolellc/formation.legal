# Formation.Legal Strategy Report

Last updated: 2026-04-26

## Executive Decision

Use `formation.legal` instead of starting from a new domain.

The old project and the new project are still topically connected: US LLC formation, registered agents, EIN, non-resident founders, and business compliance. The language and business model will change, but the topical entity is not changing. The domain is small, with roughly 20 indexed pages, but that is still better than starting from zero unless Search Console shows manual actions, security issues, or toxic backlink history.

The new site should not position itself as a formation service. It should become an evidence-based LLC decision center:

> Formation.Legal helps founders compare LLC costs, privacy risks, registered agent options, and post-formation obligations using source-backed data and hands-on research.

## Core Thesis

The opportunity is not another set of generic "how to start an LLC in [state]" articles. That market is saturated by LegalZoom, Northwest, ZenBusiness, Bizee, TRUiC, LLC University, Forbes Advisor, NerdWallet, and state government pages.

The opportunity is to own the decision layer:

- What will this LLC really cost over two years?
- Is the "free LLC" offer actually cheaper after renewals and add-ons?
- Which state creates hidden costs if I live somewhere else?
- Is my home address exposed in public records?
- Do I need a registered agent service or can I be my own agent?
- Which registered agent is best for privacy, support, and renewal price?

These topics are closer to buying intent and better aligned with Northwest Registered Agent affiliate revenue.

## Domain Migration Plan

Audit every old indexed URL before publishing the new English site.

| Old URL type | Recommended action |
|---|---|
| Turkish evergreen LLC/EIN/registered agent article with a direct English replacement | 301 redirect to the closest English page |
| Old product, order, pricing, checkout, dashboard, or support page | 410 Gone if no replacement exists |
| Turkish article that still has value | Keep under `/tr/` with self-canonical and hreflang if needed |
| Old URL with backlinks | 301 redirect to the closest matching new page |
| Irrelevant or thin page | 410 Gone or noindex if it must remain visible |

Do not redirect every old page to the homepage. That can be treated like a soft 404 and wastes topical signals.

## Brand Positioning

Formation.Legal should sound like a research desk, not a law firm and not an aggressive affiliate blog.

Preferred positioning:

- Evidence-based business formation research
- LLC cost and privacy comparisons
- Registered agent decision guides
- Public-record and compliance explainers
- General education, not legal or tax advice

Avoid:

- "We form your LLC"
- "Best legal structure for you"
- "Avoid legal trouble"
- "Guaranteed legal protection"
- "Tax-free LLC"

## Primary Audience

Start with one sharp audience, then expand.

Primary ICP:

- US-based solo founders, consultants, creators, ecommerce sellers, and small business owners who are about to form an LLC.
- Non-US founders can be a secondary cluster, but that area needs extra tax/legal caution and stronger expert review.

High-intent emotional triggers:

- "I do not want my home address public."
- "I do not understand the true cost."
- "I do not trust $0 LLC offers."
- "I am afraid of choosing the wrong state."
- "I need a simple registered agent choice."

## Content Pillars

### 1. Registered Agent Decision Hub

This is the closest pillar to Northwest affiliate conversion. The user is already evaluating whether to pay for a registered agent.

Core topics:

- Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?
- Can I be my own registered agent?
- Registered agent vs virtual address vs mailbox
- Best registered agent for privacy
- Northwest Registered Agent vs LegalZoom
- Northwest vs ZenBusiness
- LegalZoom registered agent cost

Product connection:

Northwest can be recommended where privacy, simple pricing, and registered agent renewal cost matter. Do not claim it is best for everyone.

### 2. True LLC Cost Lab

Most competitors frame formation as "$0 plus state fees." The better angle is total ownership cost.

Core topics:

- The real cost of a free LLC
- LLC cost calculator by state
- Two-year LLC cost index
- Registered agent renewal cost comparison
- EIN add-on fees compared
- Operating agreement add-on costs
- Annual report fees by state

Product connection:

Cost transparency can naturally lead to Northwest if their total cost is clearer or more predictable than competitors.

### 3. State Mythbusters

Instead of publishing 50 generic state guides first, focus on decision myths and hidden state traps.

Core topics:

- Wyoming LLC if you live in California
- Delaware LLC for small business: worth it?
- Nevada vs Wyoming LLC
- Home-state LLC vs out-of-state LLC
- California LLC $800 annual tax explained
- Delaware $300 annual LLC tax explained
- New York LLC publication requirement

Product connection:

These pages attract users before formation and can route to state-specific registered agent or formation CTAs.

### 4. Privacy and Public Records

This is a strong content moat because privacy anxiety is specific, emotional, and directly linked to registered agent services.

Core topics:

- Is my LLC address public?
- Can I use my home address for an LLC?
- How to keep your home address off business records
- Which states expose organizer/member/manager information?
- Registered agent address vs business address
- Privacy score by state

Product connection:

Northwest fits naturally when the user's main concern is privacy and public-record exposure.

### 5. Checkout and Upsell Watch

This creates the "non-commodity" proof layer. It should include screenshots, test dates, final cart prices, and methodology.

Core topics:

- We tested 5 LLC formation checkouts
- LegalZoom add-ons explained
- ZenBusiness package pricing explained
- Bizee free LLC plan: what is included and what is not
- Northwest checkout teardown
- EIN add-on fees: when to use IRS directly

Product connection:

This can become the strongest trust-building pillar if it is based on real checkout tests and regularly updated screenshots.

### 6. Post-Formation Compliance

Most formation content stops when the LLC is approved. A useful site continues into the first 30 to 90 days.

Core topics:

- What to do after LLC approval
- First 30 days after forming an LLC
- EIN, bank account, operating agreement, and licenses checklist
- Annual report deadline tracker
- Registered agent change process
- BOI reporting updates and official source tracker

Product connection:

This supports email capture, return visits, and later affiliate clicks.

## Page Template Standard

Every important page should include:

- Direct short answer in the first 100 words
- Clear affiliate disclosure near the top if monetized
- Source-backed data table
- Decision table or comparison matrix
- "Best for / not best for" section
- Methodology section for reviews, rankings, and tests
- Last reviewed date
- Official source links
- FAQ section
- Internal links to related glossary, state, comparison, and tool pages
- Legal/tax disclaimer where relevant

For AI search visibility, make answers extractable. Use definitions, tables, ordered steps, concise FAQs, and source notes. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode do not require special markup beyond normal SEO best practices, but pages must be indexable, eligible for snippets, and useful.

## Monetization Model

Primary monetization:

- Northwest Registered Agent affiliate links on registered agent, privacy, cost, and comparison pages.

Secondary monetization later:

- Email capture around checklists and calculators.
- Sponsored placements only if disclosed and separated from editorial rankings.
- Potential CPA partnerships with banks, mailboxes, bookkeeping, or tax providers after the site has trust.

Do not over-monetize early. The first goal is trust and data credibility.

## Compliance Rules

Formation.Legal should not provide personalized legal or tax advice. Use general informational language.

Safer language:

- "Common options include..."
- "In many states..."
- "This may matter if..."
- "Check your state agency or consult a licensed professional..."

Risky language:

- "You should form in Wyoming."
- "This is the best legal structure for you."
- "This will protect your assets."
- "You will not owe tax."

Every affiliate page should disclose the commission relationship clearly and close to the recommendation.
