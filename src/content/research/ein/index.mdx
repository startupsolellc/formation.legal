## Definition

An **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** is a 9-digit federal tax identification number assigned to businesses by the IRS.

Format: `XX-XXXXXXX` (same as SSN, but for business entities)

## Why EIN is Critical for Non-US Founders

EIN is the **gateway document** for:

1. **US Business Bank Accounts**: All US banks require EIN to open corporate accounts.
2. **Payment Processors**: Stripe, PayPal, and Paddle require an EIN before activation.
3. **IRS Compliance**: Foreign-owned LLCs must have an EIN for tax filings, such as Form 5472.
4. **B2B Contracts**: US companies often require an EIN on W-9 forms.

## EIN vs ITIN vs SSN

| ID | Entity | Who Needs It |
|----|--------|-------------|
| **SSN** | Personal | US citizens and residents |
| **ITIN** | Personal | Nonresident aliens with US tax filing |
| **EIN** | Business | Every US LLC, regardless of owner residency |

## How to Apply as Non-US Founder

### Option 1: Fax Form SS-4 (Recommended)

1. Download [IRS Form SS-4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fss4.pdf)
2. Complete all fields, following the instructions below.
3. Fax to IRS: **1-855-641-6935** (international) or **1-859-669-5760**
4. Receive EIN via fax within minutes (if calling) or 4-6 weeks (if mailing).

**SS-4 Key Fields for Foreign Applicants:**

| Field | What to Write |
|-------|---------------|
| **1a** | LLC's legal name (same as Articles of Organization) |
| **3** | Trade name (optional, same as legal name if none) |
| **4** | Executor, administrator, trustee, " Foreign" |
| **5a-b** | US state where LLC was formed |
| **6** | US county where LLC is located |
| **7a** | Foreign owner's name (first, middle, last) |
| **7b** | Leave blank or write "N/A" |
| **8a** | US state of LLC formation |
| **9** | Tax year end (December 31 for most) |
| **10** | Type of business: 00-0 for LLC |
| **11** | Reason for applying: "Started new business" |
| **12** | Date business started |
| **13** | Employees: "None" if pre-revenue |
| **14** | Estimated wages: 0 if none |
| **15** | First month payroll: "N/A" |

### Option 2: Online Application (US Phone Required)

The [IRS online EIN application](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) requires:
- US phone number for verification
- SSN or ITIN of responsible party

**Not available for non-US founders without US phone.**

### Option 3: Phone (May Not Work for Non-US)

Call IRS at **1-800-829-4933**:
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 7am–7pm local time
- May require SSN/ITIN verification
- Success rate is lower for non-US applicants

## After You Receive Your EIN

Save your **EIN Confirmation Letter (CP 575 or 147c)**. This is the official document banks and payment processors require.

You will need EIN for:
- Mercury or Relay bank account opening
- Stripe account verification
- PayPal business account setup
- Filing Form 5472 (if foreign-owned with gross receipts > $20,000)
