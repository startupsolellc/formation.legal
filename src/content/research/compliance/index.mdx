## Definition

**Compliance** in the context of US LLCs refers to the set of regulatory requirements that business entities must satisfy to maintain their legal standing and avoid penalties.

Unlike forming an LLC (one-time event), compliance is an ongoing obligation that spans the life of your company.

## Why Compliance Matters for Non-US Founders

Non-US founders often underestimate compliance requirements because:
- Home country regulations may differ significantly
- Initial focus is on formation and banking, not ongoing obligations
- Many compliance tasks are invisible until problems arise

**The consequences of non-compliance:**
- IRS penalties up to $25,000 per Form 5472
- State dissolution of LLC (loss of legal entity status)
- Bank account closure due to regulatory violations
- Inability to open new US bank accounts

## Key Compliance Obligations for Foreign-Owned LLCs

### 1. FinCEN BOI Reporting

The Corporate Transparency Act requires most LLCs to file Beneficial Ownership Information with FinCEN:

| Filing Type | Deadline | Penalty |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| Initial BOI report | 90 days from formation | $500 per violation |
| Updated BOI | 30 days of changes | $500 per violation |

**Who must file:** All LLCs formed in the US except exempt entities (very large companies, publicly traded firms).

**Information required:** Beneficial owner details (name, birthdate, address, ID) and company applicant information.

### 2. IRS Tax Filings

Depending on your LLC structure and revenue, you may need:

| Form | Purpose | Who Files |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Form 5472 | Foreign-owned LLC informational return | Single-member foreign-owned LLCs with $20,000+ receipts or $20,000+ assets |
| Form 1120 | C-Corp income tax return | LLCs electing C-corp taxation |
| Form 1065 | Partnership return | Multi-member LLCs |
| Form 1040-NR | Nonresident alien tax return | Non-US persons with US income |

### 3. State Annual Reports

Most states require LLCs to file annual reports and pay franchise taxes:

| State | Annual Fee | Report Deadline |
|-------|------------|-----------------|
| Delaware | $300 | March 1 |
| Wyoming | $60 | First day of anniversary month |
| California | $800 | Anniversary of formation |
| New York | $25 | Anniversary of formation |

## Compliance Timeline for New LLCs

### Year 1 Checklist

**Month 1-3:**
- File BOI report with FinCEN (90-day window)
- Apply for EIN (if not done during formation)
- Set up accounting system for tax tracking

**Month 6:**
- Review state compliance requirements
- Confirm registered agent service active
- Check for any additional state licenses needed

**Month 12:**
- File state annual report (varies by state)
- Evaluate tax filing requirements
- Update BOI if any information changed

## Compliance vs Legal: Understanding the Difference

Many non-US founders confuse compliance with legal protection. Here is the distinction:

| Aspect | Legal | Compliance |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Protect the LLC and its members | Satisfy regulatory requirements |
| One-time vs Ongoing | One-time setup | Continuous obligation |
| Failure consequence | Personal liability exposure | Penalties, dissolution |
| Primary triggers | Formation documents | Tax filings, reports |
