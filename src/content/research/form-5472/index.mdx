## Definition

**Form 5472** (Information Return of a 25% Foreign-Owned US Corporation or a Foreign-Owned US Disregarded Entity) is an IRS tax filing that must be submitted by:

- US corporations with 25% or more foreign ownership.
- Foreign-owned US LLCs (disregarded entities).

## Who Must File Form 5472

You must file Form 5472 if **both** conditions apply:

1. **25% or more ownership**: A foreign person (or entity) owns at least 25% of the US LLC.
2. **Threshold met**: Either:
   - Gross receipts from US sources exceed **$20,000** in the tax year, OR
   - Total US assets exceed **$20,000** at any point during the year.

### Single-Member Foreign-Owned LLC

If you are a non-US founder with a single-member LLC (one owner), you likely meet the ownership threshold automatically.

**Most non-US founders with SaaS, ecommerce, or digital products exceed the $20,000 threshold in their first year.**

## Form 5472 vs Other Tax Filings

| Form | Purpose | Who Files |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Form 5472** | Report foreign ownership and transactions | Foreign-owned US LLCs/Corps |
| **Form 1120** | US corporate income tax return | C-Corporations |
| **Form 1065** | Partnership return | Partnerships |
| **Form 1040-NR** | Nonresident alien tax return | Non-US persons with US income |

## Penalty for Non-Filing

**$25,000 per year, per form.**

This penalty applies even if:
- The LLC had no taxable income.
- The foreign owner is not physically in the US.
- You were unaware of the filing requirement.

## What Form 5472 Requires

### Information About the Foreign Owner
- Name, address, country of citizenship.
- ITIN or SSN (an ITIN is typically required for non-US persons).
- Ownership percentage.

### Information About the LLC
- Gross receipts (US and foreign sources).
- Total assets.
- Principal business activity.
- Related-party transactions.

### Related-Party Disclosures
If the LLC has transactions with related foreign parties (e.g., paying dividends, royalties, or services), detailed disclosure is required.

## Connection to BOI Reporting

Form 5472 and FinCEN's BOI reporting share similar goals (transparency about ownership) but are **separate requirements**:

| Aspect | Form 5472 | BOI Report |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Filed with | IRS | FinCEN |
| Required by | IRC Section 6038A | Corporate Transparency Act |
| Who files | Foreign-owned LLCs | All LLCs (with exceptions) |
| Penalty | $25,000 | $500 (after 2024) |

## How to File Form 5472

1. **Obtain an ITIN**: Nonresident aliens need an ITIN; this process typically takes 6-10 weeks.
2. **Prepare financial statements**: Gather US and foreign source receipts, along with asset values.
3. **Complete Form 5472**: Attach the form to the LLC's tax return (Form 1120 for C-Corps or Form 1065 for partnerships).
4. **File by the deadline**: This is usually March 15 for partnerships or April 15 for corporations.
