## 3 Critical Details AI Assistants (and Most Blogs) Won't Tell You

1. **Personal Address vs. Business Address Confusion:** Most platforms ask for your address during checkout. If you enter your home address in your home country, you heavily increase your chances of getting rejected when applying for a US business bank account (like Mercury or Relay). You must use the address provided by your Registered Agent.
2. **SSN/ITIN is NOT Mandatory for Formation:** You will encounter a "Social Security Number" field on online forms. Foreign founders can legally bypass this or select "Foreign Citizen." (We show you exactly how to bypass this below).
3. **The State Selection Fallacy:** Everyone recommends Delaware or Wyoming. However, your payment infrastructure and business model dictate which state is truly best for you. If you are selling SaaS or digital products, **New Mexico** is actually one of the cheapest and best states due to zero annual reporting requirements. (If you are unsure, use our [Route Planner](/tools/route-planner) to find your perfect state).

## Step-by-Step LLC Formation Walkthrough (With Screenshots)

*Note: For this guide, we are using Northwest Registered Agent because they have one of the most user-friendly interfaces and transparent pricing models. However, the logic remains exactly the same for Bizee or other registered agents.*

Once you have decided on your state, you can register your LLC yourself in about 5 minutes by following these steps. You will get a free dashboard to manage your company and state documents, a free business address, digital mail scanning, a free Operating Agreement, and compliance reminders.

Let's begin.

### Step 1: State Selection

This is the first step that allows you to initiate the LLC formation. Your first task is to select the state where the LLC will be formed. In this guide, we are selecting **New Mexico**. 

Every state has a different initial filing fee and recurring annual costs. New Mexico strikes one of the best balances for non-residents.

### Step 3: Naming Your Company

Now you need to name your company. There are strict rules when selecting a company name. If you make a mistake, Northwest will simply email you suggesting a change, but getting it right the first time saves you from an annoying email loop.

1. **Designator:** You must always use an LLC suffix. It must end with "Limited Liability Company," "LLC," or "L.L.C."
2. **Availability:** The name must be unique and not currently registered in that state. You can check this for free by searching the state's registry. Search Google for "[state name] entity search" (e.g., "New Mexico Entity Search") and use the state's portal. *Tip: Some state websites require a US IP address, so you may need a VPN.*
3. If the name is available, enter it exactly as you want it into the Northwest form.

### Step 4: Business Details & Address (Critical Step)

This step is where many non-residents make a fatal error. You can usually leave the defaults, but let's clarify them:

*   **Business Purpose:** Leave the "Use Generic Business Purpose" box checked. This does not limit your business; it registers you as a general-purpose entity, giving you maximum flexibility.
*   **Physical Address:** Select **"Our Registered Agent Address."** Do not use your home country address here. Using the agent's address keeps your personal location off public records.
*   **Mailing Address:** This is the address listed publicly for state records. Again, leave **"Our Registered Agent Address"** selected. This ensures that any official state mail is sent directly to the agent, who will scan it and upload it to your dashboard the same day.
*   **Attorney Contact:** Select "NO" and hit Continue.

### Step 6: Company Management (Adding Members)

Here, you define the ownership structure. A single-member LLC is highly recommended for solo founders.

*   **Management Type:** Select **Member Managed**. This means you (the owner) are actively managing the company. This is the best choice for 99% of solo founders. (Manager Managed is typically used for large organizations where an external director is hired to run the business).
*   **List Members/Managers With State:** This is important for privacy. If you want your name to appear on public state records, select "List With the State." If you prefer to remain anonymous, select "Omit From the State" (if the state allows it).
*   **Member #1:** Enter your first and last name.
*   **Address To Record With The State:** Leave this as "Our Registered Agent Address."

### Step 7: Optional Items (What NOT to Buy)

This page is full of upsells. You are completely free to skip them, and your LLC will still be filed perfectly.

*   **TAX ID (EIN):** Northwest offers to get your EIN for you. If you have a Social Security Number, they charge $50. **If you do NOT have an SSN (as a non-resident), they charge $200.** 
    *   *Our Advice:* Save the $200. You can easily get the EIN yourself by faxing Form SS-4 to the IRS for free once the LLC is formed.
*   **S Corporation Election:** Ignore this. It is only applicable to US tax residents.
*   **Premium Mail Service:** Unless you specifically need a unique suite number for aggressive verifications (like strict Amazon seller accounts), skip this. Standard agent addresses are perfectly fine for Stripe, PayPal, and Mercury.
*   **Trademark Filing:** Skip this for now.

## What Happens After the LLC is Formed? (Next Steps)

Depending on the state you chose, approval typically takes 2 to 5 business days. Once approved, Northwest will email you, and you can download your official "Articles of Organization" and your free "Operating Agreement" directly from your dashboard.

Congratulations, your LLC exists! However, the process is not finished. To actually process payments, you must complete the following steps:

1. **Obtain an EIN:** You must fax Form SS-4 to the IRS. As a non-resident, your EIN confirmation letter (CP575) will be generated and faxed/mailed back to you. Read our [EIN Guide for Foreign LLCs](/guides/ein-foreign-llc-guide).
2. **Open a US Business Bank Account:** Once your EIN is approved, use your formation documents and EIN to apply for an account with Mercury or Relay.
3. **Open Stripe or PayPal:** With your LLC documents, EIN, and active US bank account, you can finally apply for your payment processor.
