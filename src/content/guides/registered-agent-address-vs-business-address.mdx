## The Short Answer

The truth about registered agent addresses is more nuanced than most guides say:

- **EIN:** RA addresses ARE accepted by the IRS — this is standard practice
- **Stripe:** RA addresses are rejected, but your home country physical address (with proof) works
- **PayPal:** RA addresses work, but connecting from restricted-country IPs triggers verification
- **Amazon:** RA addresses work only if unique — shared addresses are rejected

Most guides are overly pessimistic about RA addresses or fail to mention the real requirements. This guide is based on real-world non-US founder experience.

---

## What Is a Registered Agent Address — And What It's For

A registered agent is a person or company designated to receive legal and official documents on behalf of your LLC. The registered agent's address is where these documents are delivered.

**What your RA address receives:**
- Service of process (lawsuits, legal summons)
- State correspondence (annual report reminders, compliance notices)
- IRS notices (if sent to the LLC's registered address)
- Official government notifications

**Legal requirements for an RA address:**
- Must be a physical address (no P.O. boxes)
- Must be located in the state where your LLC is registered
- Must be accessible during normal business hours

**Your RA address is public record.** Anyone can search for it through the secretary of state's business database. This is why many non-US founders use RA services — to keep their home address off public filings.

## The Three Address Layers Every Non-US Founder Must Understand

A US LLC has **three separate address layers**, not just one:

### Layer 1: Registered Agent Address
**Purpose:** Receiving legal and government documents
**Visibility:** Public — listed on secretary of state records
**Used for:** State LLC filings, service of process, IRS correspondence
**Who provides this:** Your registered agent service

### Layer 2: Physical / KYC Address (for founder)
**Purpose:** Identity verification (KYC) with banks, payment processors, and government agencies
**Visibility:** Private — used only for verification
**Used for:** Bank account applications, Stripe, PayPal, Amazon verification
**Who provides this:** Your actual residential address in your home country
**Proof accepted:** Utility bill, bank statement, or credit card statement

### Layer 3: Business Operating Address
**Purpose:** Where your business operates (if different from home)
**Visibility:** Semi-public
**Used for:** Business correspondence, website, customer-facing addresses
**Who provides this:** Virtual office, coworking space, or your home

## EIN Application — RA Addresses Work Fine

**This is standard practice and works reliably.**

The IRS accepts registered agent addresses for EIN filings. When you apply for an EIN online or by fax, the RA address can be listed as the LLC's mailing address. The IRS sends all correspondence to this address.

Why this matters:
- You do not need a separate mailing address
- All IRS notices (including letters about Form 5472, BOI, etc.) go to your RA
- This is how most formation providers handle EIN applications

If you are a non-US founder without a US residential address, using the RA address for EIN is actually the cleaner option.

## Stripe — RA Addresses Rejected, But Home Country Address Works

### What Changed

As of May 28, 2025, Stripe explicitly rejects registered agent addresses for account verification.

> "This means Stripe can no longer allow addresses for registered agents, mailbox services, or virtual address services."

### What Actually Works

**Your home country physical address works for Stripe.**

Stripe accepts physical addresses outside the US for KYC verification. You need to provide proof:
- **Utility bill** (electricity, gas, water)
- **Bank statement** (showing your name and address)
- **Credit card statement** (showing your name and address)

These documents must show your current address and be dated within the last 90 days.

### What This Means for Non-US Founders

You do not need a US address for Stripe. If you live in Turkey, Germany, or anywhere else, your physical address there with supporting documentation is accepted.

This is a significant finding — most guides assume you need a US address for Stripe verification. You do not.

## Bank Accounts — Use Your Home Country Physical Address

Banks require a physical address for KYC (Know Your Customer) verification. This address must be where you actually operate your business.

**Your home country address works for bank accounts.**

Banks accept:
- Utility bills (electricity, gas, internet)
- Bank statements
- Credit card statements

These documents verify that you have a legitimate physical presence at the address you claim.

**RA addresses are not accepted** for bank account applications because banks need to verify that a real business operates at the address — not just that legal mail can be received at a RA service.

## PayPal — RA Addresses Work, But IP Address Matters

### RA Addresses Are Accepted

PayPal accepts registered agent addresses as your company address. You can list your RA address in your PayPal business account settings.

### The IP Problem

However, PayPal has an additional verification layer: **your IP address**.

If you connect to PayPal.com from an IP address associated with a restricted country, PayPal may trigger an address verification request — asking for a utility bill showing your address.

This happens even if your RA address and company address are perfectly valid. The trigger is your connection's IP address, not the addresses on your account.

### The Solution

**Use a US IP address when connecting to PayPal.**

This means:
- Use a VPS server with a US IP address
- Use a quality US proxy service
- Access PayPal from a US-based connection

When you connect from a US IP, PayPal does not trigger the additional utility bill verification. Your RA address and company address remain accepted without further proof.

**Important:** Do not access PayPal from your home country IP if that country is on PayPal's restricted list. The verification request is triggered by the IP, not by your nationality or company structure.

## Amazon — RA Addresses Work Only If Unique

### The Shared Address Problem

Amazon is more strict than PayPal about address verification. While Amazon accepts registered agent addresses, it specifically rejects addresses that are **shared across thousands of companies**.

If your RA service uses a commercial address that is listed for many companies (which is common with budget RA services), Amazon will reject it.

### What Amazon Accepts

Amazon accepts:
- **Premium virtual office addresses** with a unique suite number (e.g., "123 Main St, Suite 456")
- **Premium mail forwarding services** that provide a unique address with suite number
- **Dedicated addresses** that are not shared with thousands of other companies

Amazon does NOT accept:
- Shared commercial RA addresses (used by thousands of companies)
- Standard PO Box or mailbox service addresses
- Addresses that appear on multiple LLC filings

### The Practical Approach

For Amazon seller accounts:
1. Do not use a budget RA service's shared address
2. Use a premium virtual office service (like Davinci Virtual, Intelligent Office, or similar)
3. Get a unique suite number that is dedicated to your company
4. This address will be accepted for Amazon seller verification

## FAQ: Address Questions from Non-US Founders

### Can I use my registered agent address for EIN?

Yes. The IRS accepts RA addresses for EIN applications. This is standard practice and works reliably.

### My Stripe account was flagged — what do I do?

Update your address to your home country physical address (where you actually live). Provide a utility bill, bank statement, or credit card statement as proof. Stripe accepts non-US addresses for verification.

### Can I use my home country address for Stripe and banks?

Yes. Your actual residential address in your home country, with proof (utility bill, bank statement, or credit card statement), is accepted by both Stripe and most US banks.

### PayPal is asking for a utility bill — why?

You likely connected to PayPal from a restricted-country IP address. To avoid this in the future, use a US IP (VPS or proxy) when accessing PayPal.

### Amazon rejected my RA address — what do I do?

Your RA address is probably shared across thousands of companies. Use a premium virtual office service that provides a unique address with a dedicated suite number instead.

### Can I use the same address for everything?

No. You need:
- RA address for state filings and IRS
- Home country address for KYC with Stripe, banks, and PayPal
- Unique address (virtual office with suite number) for Amazon
