**The Non-US Founder's Payment Stack Guide**

Everything you need to know about building a payment infrastructure for your US LLC — without relying on a single point of failure.

This guide covers:

- **EIN Application** — Phone, fax, and mail methods for foreign LLCs (the online app doesn't work for non-US applicants)
- **Stripe in 2026** — The May 2025 address policy change that rejected thousands of registered agent addresses, and what actually works now
- **PayPal Setup** — Why your IP address matters more than your company address, and how to avoid the verification trap
- **US Banking** — Mercury and Relay requirements for non-US founders
- **Merchant of Record** — Paddle and Lemon Squeezy as tax-compliant alternatives
- **Building Your Stack** — Diversification strategies for risk management

**Updated May 2026** with current requirements.
